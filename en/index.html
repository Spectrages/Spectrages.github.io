Before the dive

A few clarifications.

Is this a real transport company?+

It is a fictional company within an art project. The project borrows the serious language of business presentations to explore the absurd logic of efficiency. The whales, passenger manifests and fuel calculations are all part of the satire.

Can I contribute money now?+

The campaign is in preparation. This website does not accept payments. A platform link, participation terms, a schedule and a detailed budget will be published before launch.

What does the $200,000 goal mean?+

It is the stated target for a future campaign to prepare and develop the art project's ecosystem. It is not an amount already raised. A final cost breakdown will be presented before funding begins.
